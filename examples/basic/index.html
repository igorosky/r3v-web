        Visibility is global for the renderer: a change made in one client reaches the other through the renderer's snapshot. A checkbox shows what the renderer displays; a pending request is marked "hiding…" or "showing…", and clicking again resends it. Without an answer, for example while a client's control channel is disconnected, the request is dropped after 5 s; reconnecting resynchronises the client from a fresh snapshot. The demo renderer has no camera, so "Focus" in client D frames the element in the image for a moment.
